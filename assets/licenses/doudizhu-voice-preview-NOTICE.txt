Dou Dizhu audition previews (2026-11-05)

Files under assets/doudizhu/voice-previews retain the audition/selection record.
The user selected the brief male and female styles for two complete runtime
profiles; the preview files themselves are excluded from Godot by .gdignore.

current-male.wav concatenates seven existing generated clips from this project.
brief-female.wav and brief-male.wav are newly synthesized short Chinese calls
using the already available Kokoro-82M-v1.1-zh model and sherpa-onnx runtime.
No new third-party recording is represented as a human voice recording here.

Audio is mono PCM16 WAV, 24 kHz. The two new takes use speaker IDs 3 (zf_001)
and 59 (zm_010), speed 1.0. Phrase wording/punctuation differs; there is no
post-production pitch shifting or time-stretching. The samples are trimmed,
peak-normalized and endpoint-faded, with 0.4 second gaps between phrases.
Exact text, segment timing and resulting SHA-256 are recorded in
doudizhu-voice-preview-manifest.json.

These samples have passed format, non-silence and hash checks. The user selected
the two brief styles after listening; that is not a claim that all 120 resulting
runtime clips have individually undergone a human listening review.

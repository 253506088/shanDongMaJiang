Qingyu Bluff — generated offline speech, 2026-11-06

256 PCM WAV clips (128 male + 128 female) generated with Kokoro-82M-v1.1-zh
and sherpa-onnx, using the pinned model identity and hashes recorded in
bluff-voice-manifest.json. These are model-generated utterances, not recordings
copied from a commercial game and not human voice acting.

Model: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M-v1.1-zh
Runtime: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx
Model and runtime: Apache License 2.0.
The full Apache-2.0 text is shipped alongside this notice as
doudizhu-Apache-2.0.txt; the pinned model card is doudizhu-Kokoro-model-card.md.

Regeneration: tools/generate_bluff_audio.py (development only).
The game uses local WAV files; it needs neither the model nor a network service.
Playback concatenates the declared quantity and rank, never the hidden faces.
